MSc · ENGR7004

Crash Modelling of a Carbon–Flax EV Battery Enclosure

Explicit LS-DYNA model of a 2.1 m hybrid carbon–flax enclosure under 1,300 kg, 13.86 m/s front and side impacts against a 5 mm deflection limit. Unprotected it deflected 209 mm (front) and 104.6 mm (side). I screened five attenuator concepts and simulated an aramid honeycomb in both load cases, bringing deflection inside the limit in the model.
